Our Companion Services

Tailored assistance designed specifically to restore independence, comfort, and security to senior citizens.

Medical & Hospital Visits

Assistance during registrations, navigating medical offices, keeping notes of prescriptions, and secure transit home.

Shopping & Outdoors

Escorts for grocery shopping, malls, parks, and carrying heavy items while ensuring safe, stable navigation.

Travel & Transit

Guiding seniors through railway stations, bus terminals, airports, managing luggage, and booking transport.

Routine & Banking

Accompanying senior citizens to banks, post offices, government buildings, and assisting in basic procedures.

How ElderGo+ Works

A simple, transparent process built with the technical ease needed for elderly users.

1

Create Profile

Register as a Senior/Family or Companion and set up safety information.

2

Book Service

3
